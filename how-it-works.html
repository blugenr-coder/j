How it works

One place to practise, print, track and improve

Most worksheet sites stop at the download. The point of WorksheetHub is that the same exercise carries through the whole loop.

Find

Search in plain language — “fractions grade 6” — or filter by grade, subject, topic, difficulty, question type and length.

Practice

Work online with eight question types, or print a worksheet and use a pencil. Same exercise, same questions, your choice.

Check

Marking is instant and forgiving about formatting. A wrong answer offers another go and a hint before it ever shows you the working.

Improve

Accuracy, streak and per-topic strength are tracked, so the next session can start where you are weakest instead of where you left off.

Every question type, marked properly

Different questions need different interactions. Multiple choice is not enough for maths, and a text box is not enough for a matching task.

What “marked properly” means

  • 0.5, 1/2 and 50% all count as the same value.
  • “x = 5” is accepted where the answer is 5.
  • Case, spacing and currency symbols are ignored; meaning is not.
  • Multiple-answer questions require every correct option and no wrong ones.
  • Written responses are never auto-marked — you compare against a sample answer.

On the printed sheet

  • Name, date and score fields at the top.
  • Space to show working on calculation questions.
  • Answer lines, tick boxes or blank grids as the question needs.
  • An answer key with worked explanations — on its own page, so it can be kept back.
  • Up to three copies at once, and hints optional.

Print it the moment you need paper

A homework sheet, a cover lesson, a revision session with no screens. Every exercise in the library produces a clean A4 worksheet with a separate key.
