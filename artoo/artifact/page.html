    How it builds a model

    • Background. With AI tools on, Segment Anything (SlimSAM, on your device) separates the character from the background. AI depth tells it where to look and drops walls and panels that sit far behind. One click in "Fix the background" corrects it. Without AI, a plain background is flooded away from the edges.
    • Shape. A Poisson solve inside the outline gives every part a round cross-section sized to its own width. AI depth (Depth Anything V2, on your device) pushes forward what is nearer. The back is predicted the way a modeller blocks it out: big forms (a head, a belly) carry round, small details stay on the front. A side photo, if you add one, replaces the guess with the real profile.
    • Surface. Marching cubes extracts the volume; Taubin smoothing removes the stair-steps without shrinking it.
    • Texture. The front takes the picture's colours. The back and the sides take the character's main colours: eyes, mouths and logos stay on the front instead of being mirrored onto the back.
    • Chat. Claude sees your picture and the model, and uses the studio's own controls: it rebuilds with new settings, corrects the cutout, and adds simple parts (a weapon, horns, a brim) the picture cannot give.
    • Copy check. The model is rendered from the photo's viewpoint and scored on outline, side profile, relief and smoothness; Auto-copy searches the settings for the best score.

    What "learning" means here

    Each candidate uses a different shape recipe. Your stars update a running estimate for each recipe (Thompson sampling), so good recipes get picked more often and untried ones still get a turn. Artoo does not retrain a neural network.

    Tips: one character or object, filling most of the frame, at least 512 pixels across. Turn AI tools on for anything but a plain background. Thin parts (staffs, cables, fingers) are often lost; ask the chat to add them back. For a side view, keep the same distance and turn the object to face right.
