    How it builds a model

    • Cutout. The plain background is flooded away from the edges; transparent PNGs use their alpha.
    • Shape. A Poisson solve inside the outline gives every part a round cross-section sized to its own width. A side photo replaces that guess with the real profile and carves the volume.
    • Surface. Marching cubes extracts the volume; Taubin smoothing removes the stair-steps without shrinking it.
    • Texture. Each face takes colour from the photo that faces it, with colours bled past the outline so edges stay clean.

    What "learning" means here

    Each candidate uses a different shape recipe. Your stars update a running estimate for each recipe (Thompson sampling), so good recipes get picked more often and untried ones still get a turn. Artoo does not retrain a neural network.

    Tips: one object, filling most of the frame, on a plain background that contrasts with it, lit evenly. For a side view, keep the same distance and turn the object to face right.
