      PW-01 / GOVERNMENT CONTRACTOR

      PADWORKS

      Build rockets on a public budget. Fly the contract. Bring it home.

      Progress saves on this device · Works with touch or keyboard & mouse

      OPERATING MANUAL

      1. Read the contract at the terminal. Each contract has required objectives, a bonus, and a budget.
      2. Design a blueprint. Stack parts top to bottom. The review panel flags build flaws: engines firing into parts, fins mounted too high, buried parachutes, misplaced heat shields. Flaws have real effects in flight.
      3. Buy parts new, or cheaper and worn on the used market. Deliveries drop into the yellow DELIVERY bay.
      4. Build it with your hands. Grab parts and place them on the pad, bottom first. The hologram shows the next part. The crane can auto-assemble if it is running.
      5. Launch at the red console. Stage to ignite, steer, handle failures as they happen.
      6. Land. Parachutes need speed under 300 m/s. Legs raise the safe touchdown speed. Coming back from orbit without a heat shield at the bottom is fatal.
      7. Success unlocks the next contract and a larger grant. Failure means repeating it on a reduced grant. Recovered parts can be resold or flown again.

      Desktop

      Click to capture the mouse. WASD move, mouse look, E grab / place / use, Q throw, T terminal, Esc menu.
      Flight: A/D rotate, W/S throttle, Z/X full/cut, Space stage, P chute, , . time warp, E act on failures, 1-3 choose.

      Touch

      Left thumb: move stick. Right thumb: look. Context button grabs, places and uses.
      Flight: drag the throttle, hold ◀ ▶ to rotate, drag empty space to orbit the camera.
